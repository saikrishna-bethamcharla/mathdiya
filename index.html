Frequently Asked Questions

Everything US parents need to know about our curriculum, cohorts, and schedules.

R.S. Aggarwal is widely recognized as the gold standard for conceptual math rigor. While many US school classrooms only cover procedural calculations, R.S. Aggarwal breaks every topic into step-by-step logical building blocks. When combined with our Texas STAAR test prep (question decoding, grid-in mechanics, and timed problem solving), students gain both deep mathematical intuition and high exam confidence.
Research proves that isolated 1-on-1 tutoring creates fatigue, whereas large classrooms leave quieter students behind. In small pods of 5 to 8 students, our certified instructors maintain direct eyes on every single child's digital notebook, while students learn from seeing different ways their peers solve identical problems.
Spring 2027 is the last year Texas administers a single end-of-year STAAR exam before switching to three shorter progress tests during the year. Because our classes focus on foundational math comprehension rather than short-term cramming, students build the problem-solving intuition required for either format.
Every live session is recorded in HD and uploaded to your parent portal alongside the class whiteboard notes within 2 hours. Parents can also request a 15-minute quick catch-up with the teacher via WhatsApp.
